## 🎯 About The Project

Traditional SEO suites (like Ahrefs or Semrush) are bloated, complicated, and lock users into hefty $100+/month subscriptions. **Light SEO Suite** takes a micro-utility approach: single-purpose, highly efficient browser extensions that perform specific, high-leverage SEO tasks directly from your current tab.

All utilities in the suite are cataloged, searchable, and maintained at our central directory: **[seo-tools-index.pages.dev](https://seo-tools-index.pages.dev)**.

---

## ⚡ What You Can Do With The Suite

Through the central hub, users gain access to a growing collection of modular micro-tools covering the essential workflow of launching and scaling organic search traffic:

- **Competitor Reverse-Engineering**: Inspect competitor domains, analyze ranking structures, and track keyword velocity in real time.
- **Blue Ocean Keyword Discovery**: Filter out ultra-competitive search queries and instantly extract underserved, high-opportunity keyword pockets with 0–100 opportunity scoring.
- **Side-by-Side SEO Auditing**: Compare on-page architecture, content density, DOM structure, and heading hierarchies directly between two competing URLs.
- **SERP & Market Intelligence**: Export clean, structured CSV datasets across multiple regional Google search engines with zero manual scraping overhead.

---

## 💎 Platform Architecture: One Unified Account

Every extension published under the suite is connected via a decentralized yet unified backend architecture:

1. **Shared Credit Balance**: You only need one single account. Credits purchased or earned are synchronized instantly across every active and upcoming tool in the ecosystem.
2. **No Recurring Subscriptions**: Built on a transparent pay-as-you-go credit pool. No monthly retainers, no automatic credit expirations, and zero subscription lock-ins.
3. **Zero-Bloat & Privacy First**: Extensions operate strictly on minimal permissions—no background activity tracking, no keystroke monitoring, and no persistent browser memory drain.
