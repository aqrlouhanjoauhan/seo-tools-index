BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Security

Engineered for complete developer autonomy, zero vendor lock-in, and uncompromising data privacy.

No Account or Registration Required

You never register an account with us. Simply enter your official DataForSEO API Login and Password directly into the extension popup. Start running live queries immediately.

Zero Server Intermediaries

All API queries are initiated locally from your browser sandbox directly to https://api.dataforseo.com/v3/ via HTTPS Basic Auth. Your credentials and search histories are never routed through or stored on third-party backend servers.

Direct Pay-As-You-Go Billing

No markup, no commission fees, and no recurring credit cards on our site. Your API costs are deducted strictly from your own DataForSEO account at official wholesale rates.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. BYOK Architecture: All extensions operate under a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model. Users supply their own DataForSEO API credentials, which are stored solely within the local Chrome browser storage (chrome.storage.local).

2. Direct Network Transmission: HTTP requests are dispatched directly from the client's browser environment to the official DataForSEO API servers (api.dataforseo.com). No query parameters, payload bodies, or authentication tokens are routed through, inspected by, or saved on our servers.

3. Zero Tracking & Analytics: We do not track browsing history, keystrokes, clipboard entries, or personal identity information. No third-party behavioral analytics or advertising trackers are bundled inside our software.
